# Backbone — Routine Reference

Back-pain-aware training plan for chronic right-side lower back pain (scoliosis). Evidence-informed: anti-movement core work (McGill-style), hip mobility, glute strengthening, load management. **Not medical advice.** Every exercise is pain-guided: mild discomfort or effort is fine; sharp, radiating, or worsening pain means stop, swap, or scale down. Worth showing this document to a physio.

Global rules:

- **No deadlifts or hinge-under-load variations.** Not programmed anywhere.
- **No treadmill running.** Cardio is bike or elliptical (rower only with caution — it loads the lumbar spine in flexion; skip if unsure).
- **Axial-loading flag.** Exercises marked ⚠️ compress the spine even when done safely. The app's "skip axial loading" toggle auto-substitutes them with their first non-axial alternative.
- **Warm-up is movement prep, not cardio.** Plus a light "ramp-in" first set (~50-60% working weight) on each strength exercise.
- Full-body sessions with alternating **Day A / Day B** — every session stands alone, whatever the weekly frequency.

---

## Movement prep (start of every gym session, 3-4 min)

| Exercise | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| Cat-camel | ×8 slow | Gentle full range, no forcing end-range |
| Glute bridge | ×10 | Squeeze glutes, don't arch the lower back |
| Bird dog | ×6/side | Slow, hips level, reach long not high |
| Bodyweight squat or leg swings | ×8 / ×10 per leg | Easy depth, just greasing the pattern |

---

## Gym — Regular day

Session sizes: **Short ~25min** = first 3 strength exercises × 2 sets + 1 core. **Medium ~45min** = first 5 × 3 sets + 2 core + 8-10 min cardio finisher. **Long ~60-75min** = all 6 × 3 sets + 3 core + 15-20 min cardio finisher + optional full stretch routine.

Default strength dose: 3 sets × 8-12 reps, ~90s rest. Progress by adding reps to 12, then adding weight and dropping back to 8.

### Day A

| # | Exercise | Flags | Alternatives (back-safe swaps) | Cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leg press | | single-leg leg press · leg extension | Feet mid-platform, don't let the lower back round off the pad at depth |
| 2 | Chest press (machine or DB bench) | | DB bench press · push-ups | Shoulder blades set, feet planted |
| 3 | Seated cable row | | chest-supported machine row · single-arm DB row (hand on bench) | Tall neutral spine, pull to belly, no torso heave |
| 4 | DB hip thrust / glute bridge | | bodyweight or single-leg glute bridge · glute kickback machine | Posterior tilt at top, squeeze glutes, ribs down — don't hyperextend the back |
| 5 | Lat pulldown | | assisted pull-up machine · straight-arm pulldown | Slight lean OK, no lumbar arch-yank |
| 6 | Cable Pallof press | | dead bug · bird dog | Resist rotation, breathe, hips square |

**Core (Day A pool, in order used by S/M/L):** front plank (30-45s) → bird dog ×8/side → Pallof press (if not done in strength).

### Day B

| # | Exercise | Flags | Alternatives | Cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet or guided (Smith) squat | ⚠️ axial | **leg press** · DB split squat (light DBs at sides) | Chest tall, sit between the hips, depth stays pain-free |
| 2 | Incline DB press | | incline machine press · chest press machine | 30-45° incline, feet planted |
| 3 | Seated row, wide grip | | wide machine row · face pull | Elbows high-ish, squeeze mid-back |
| 4 | Seated or lying leg curl | | the other leg curl variant · single-leg curl | Control the negative |
| 5 | Seated DB shoulder press (back supported) | ⚠️ axial | **cable lateral raise + face pull** · machine lateral raise | Back against pad, don't flare ribs |
| 6 | Hip abduction machine | | cable glute kickback · banded clamshells · side-lying leg raises | Slow, feel it in the side of the hip |

**Core (Day B pool):** side planks 20-40s/side (slight extra volume on the weaker side, pain-guided) → dead bug ×10 → bird dog ×8/side.

### Cardio finisher

Bike or elliptical, any intensity that feels right (steady or intervals). Log duration + intensity + optional kcal.

## Gym — Cardio Focus (~45-60 min)

A first-class session for cardio-leaning days:

1. Movement prep (3-4 min)
2. **1-2 strength/core exercises × 3 sets** — the app rotates a suggestion from: leg press · DB hip thrust · seated cable row · chest press · side planks · Pallof press (any can be swapped)
3. **30-45 min bike/elliptical** — steady or variable intervals, your call (intensity chips: easy / moderate / intervals / hard, optional kcal)
4. 3-5 min hip mobility (lizard, 90/90, figure-4)

Honest framing: strength ~2×/week is the main pain-reduction driver; cardio-focus days are additive on top, great for weight loss and general health, and never a "lesser" session.

---

## Gym — Pain day (variant of every session type)

No loaded strength. Moving today still counts.

1. Gentle warm-up: cat-camel ×8, easy walking, gentle bird dog if tolerated
2. **Easy low-impact cardio**: Short 15 min · Medium 25 min · Long 40 min (bike/elliptical, conversational pace)
3. Optional pain-tolerated isometrics: bird dog ×6/side, gentle side plank (knees-down scaling) — skip freely
4. Extended mobility: the stretch routine below, gentle end-ranges only

## Stretch routine (standalone or post-gym)

Timer-guided. **Full ~12-15 min** = all steps. **Short ~7 min** = steps marked ●.

| # | Stretch | Dose | Cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ● | Cat-camel | ×8 slow | Spine warm-up, no end-range forcing |
| 2 | Child's pose with side reach | 45s/side | Sink hips back, walk hands to each side, breathe into the tight side |
| 3 ● | **Lizard pose** | 60s/side | Back knee down is fine, sink the hips, front knee tracks out |
| 4 ● | 90/90 hip switch + hold | 45s/side | Tall chest over the front shin |
| 5 ● | Figure-4 glute stretch (lying) | 45s/side | Pull the leg in gently, head down |
| 6 ● | Hip flexor / couch stretch | 45s/side | Tuck the pelvis first, then shift forward slightly — small motion, big stretch |
| 7 | Hamstring stretch (strap or ledge) | 45s/side | Knee soft, hinge from the hip |
| 8 | Open-book thoracic rotation | ×8/side | Rotate from the mid-back, hips stacked and still |
| 9 | QL side-bend stretch | 30s/side | Gentle — this one targets near the painful area; back off if it bites |

**Optional core add-on** (their physio work — a home for non-gym days): dead bug ×10 → bird dog ×8/side → front plank 30-45s → side planks 20-40s/side.

## Progression & flare-up guidance

- **When pain is low (0-3):** normal training; nudge weights up when 12 reps feel easy.
- **Moderate (4-5):** the app suggests the pain-day variant; overriding to a regular session is fine — favor machines, drop axial work, stop any set that provokes symptoms.
- **High (6+):** pain-day variant or stretch-only. Easy movement usually beats total rest, but nothing is mandatory.
- **Trend matters more than any single day.** The History screen exists to show the long arc: pain trend down, weights up, sessions accumulating — gently.
